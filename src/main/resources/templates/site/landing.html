    Questions

    Is my patients' data kept separate from other clinics?

    Yes. Every clinic has its own database. There is no shared patient table, and signing in decides which clinic you can see.

    Do patients need to install an app?

    No. Patients use WhatsApp, which they already have. Staff use the web app in any browser.

    Which languages does the WhatsApp assistant speak?

    English, Hindi, Telugu and Tamil. Each patient's choice is remembered, including for reminders and queue alerts.

    Do we need our own WhatsApp number?

    Yes — your clinic uses its own WhatsApp Business number, so messages come from your clinic's name. We help you connect it.

    Who pays for the WhatsApp messages?

    WhatsApp Business messaging is billed by Meta to the clinic's own WhatsApp Business account, according to Meta's pricing. We can walk you through what to expect.

    Can we try it before deciding?

    Yes. Request a demo and we set up a trial clinic for you, with sample doctors if you like.

    Can several doctors and staff use it at once?

    Yes. Doctors, front desk, pharmacy and admin each sign in with their own account and see only what their role needs.

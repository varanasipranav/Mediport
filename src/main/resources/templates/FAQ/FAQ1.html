How to create price approval request?
Follow the link. Price Approval-->Create. Fill the details and click on continue
How to view the request list/report?
Follow the link. Price Approval-->List.
How to approve/reject the request?
Click on below tab in dashboard.
Choose the document.
Click on Approve/Reject to act on document.
How to post request to SAP?
Click on below tab in dashboard.
Choose the document.
Click on Approve/Reject to act on document.
How to reset password?
Click on "Forgot Your Password" link in login page
Give your registered email id and click on Reset. Email link will be sent your email.
